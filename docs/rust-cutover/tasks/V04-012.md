Milestone: v0.4.0 Binance Sandbox Product Foundation
Priority: P0

Risk: medium

## Goal

Produce the final v0.4.0 readiness report after the queue completes.

Plain language: publish one strict PASS/FAIL document that says whether the
Binance sandbox product foundation is ready to release.

## Dependencies

- Create the final v0.4 readiness report.
- Cite `V04-001` through `V04-011` evidence.
- Record release-gate and local verification results.
- State clearly what v0.4 proves and what remains out of scope.

## Non-goals

- Do not create a release tag.
- Do not publish a GitHub Release.
- Do not declare production trading readiness.
- Do not expand into later milestones inside this report.

## Implementation guidance

The report must separate three things cleanly: what v0.4 claims, what the
verification actually proves, and what remains explicitly deferred after the
release.

## Verification criteria

- Readiness report cites `V04-001` through `V04-011`.
- Report states explicit PASS or FAIL.
- Report keeps real funds and production trading out of scope.
