# V04-009 - risk rejection smoke

Milestone: v0.4.0 Binance Sandbox Product Foundation
Priority: P0
Owner role: Risk Agent
Review role: Verification & Release Gatekeeper
Risk: medium

## Goal

Prove deterministic risk rejection on the v0.4 sandbox path.

Plain language: the system must be able to say "this order was rejected by
risk" with clear local evidence before it claims business-facing state panels.

## Dependencies

- `V04-008`

## Scope

- Add a deterministic risk rejection smoke on the Binance sandbox path.
- Surface rejection reason and evidence clearly.
- Keep the smoke local, fixture-backed, and non-production.

## Non-goals

- Do not productize a full risk service.
- Do not claim production pre-trade parity.
- Do not require real accounts or real balances.

## Implementation guidance

The important part is observability and determinism. Users and later dashboard
panels need a stable rejection summary, not just an internal failure.

## Verification criteria

- Risk rejection smoke runs locally.
- Rejection reason is explicit and deterministic.
- Smoke stays inside the Binance sandbox boundary.
