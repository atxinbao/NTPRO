## Evidence Required Before A Later Mutation Version

A later owner-approved mutation release must not be considered until evidence
exists for all of these:

- owner-approved scope naming exact venue, environment, account boundary,
  symbols, order type, quantity/notional limit, and stop conditions;
- fail-closed release gate proving default CI and local runs do not submit,
  cancel, replace, amend, retry, correct, or reconnect production orders;
- long-running shadow session heartbeat, stop, and stale-data behavior;
- owner-run production online read-only proof pack with redaction;
- kill-switch dry-run artifact and manual approval artifact;
- Decimal/string-only amount boundary for risk and execution preflight fields;
- Dashboard trader/ops role boundary with no production order controls;
- risk rejection, reconciliation, and golden trace evidence for the proposed
  mutation path;
- explicit rollback and incident evidence for the proposed mutation path.
