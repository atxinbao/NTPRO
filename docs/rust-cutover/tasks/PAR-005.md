# PAR-005 Partial/Full Fill Account-Balance Regression

Date: 2026-07-29
Executor: Codex
GitHub issue: #1173
Risk: high
Classification: separately-scoped-runtime-maintenance

## Goal

Replace the ignored empty account-balance placeholder with an executable
partial/full fill regression at the component that owns cash balance and order
reservation updates.

中文摘要：历史空测试位于 RiskEngine，但现金账户余额、手续费和订单锁定资金由
`AccountsManager` 更新。本任务把回归迁移到真实所有权位置，并修复部分成交后
仍按原始订单数量锁定资金的问题。

## Scope

- remove the ignored empty placeholder from the risk integration test;
- replay submitted, accepted, partial-fill, and final-fill order events;
- assert account total, locked, free, and cumulative commission balances;
- reserve cash against order leaves quantity after a partial fill;
- release the reservation after the final fill;
- update both ignored-test registers until PAR-011 unifies them.

## Non-Goals

- no margin-account or betting-account reservation change;
- no realized PnL, mark-price, venue-send, retry, remediation, or live-exchange
  capability claim;
- no public API change;
- no edit to frozen `docs/rust-cutover/release/v0_32_0_*` files.

## Behavior Contract

1. A cash limit buy for 100,000 at 0.80000 initially reserves USD 80,000.
2. A partial fill of 40,000 with USD 8 commission leaves USD 48,000 reserved.
3. The partial-fill account total is USD 967,992 and free balance is
   USD 919,992 after recalculating the remaining reservation.
4. A final fill of 60,000 with USD 12 commission releases all reservations.
5. The final account total and free balance are USD 919,980, with cumulative
   commission of USD 20.

## Acceptance

- the historical test name runs by default under AccountsManager ownership;
- no account-balance placeholder remains in the risk test;
- the exact event sequence and balance assertions pass in standard and high
  precision;
- complete portfolio/risk tests, Clippy, golden replay, ignored-test scan, risk
  inventory, current governance, backend freeze, and fast smoke pass;
- independent review and hosted checks approve the high-risk change.
