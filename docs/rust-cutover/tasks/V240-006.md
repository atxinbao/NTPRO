## Goal

Define a retry / no-retry policy ledger for v0.24.0 order control. The ledger
prevents implicit retry and records replayable retry decisions with policy,
scope, reason, prior-attempt, owner approval, and audit evidence.

Plain Chinese summary: 本任务只定义 retry / no-retry policy ledger 证据。默认
不允许隐式 retry；任何 retry preview 都必须绑定 policy、scope、原因、prior
attempt、owner approval 和 audit ref；不自动重试真实订单，也不实现生产 retry
scheduler。

## Scope

- Define retry eligibility categories: transport error, timeout, unknown state,
  business rejection, and risk rejection.
- Define no-retry terminal categories.
- Record retry intent digest, prior attempt ref, policy approval, owner
  approval requirement, and audit ref.
- Cover duplicate retry, missing prior attempt, unknown state retry blocked,
  and policy mismatch cases.

## Non-Goals

- Do not automatically retry real orders.
- Do not implement a production retry scheduler.
- Do not call execution adapters or exchange endpoints.
- Do not mutate production order state.
- Do not add Dashboard live order controls.

## Done Criteria

- No implicit retry is allowed by default.
- Retry requires explicit policy, owner approval, audit ref, prior attempt, and
  matching scope.
- Terminal business and risk rejections produce no-retry evidence.
- Duplicate retry, missing prior attempt, unknown state, and policy mismatch
  fail closed.
- No runtime trading behavior, network call, adapter call, production order
  mutation, retry scheduler, or Dashboard operation control is added.
