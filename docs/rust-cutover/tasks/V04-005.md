# V04-005 - Binance fixture market data replay

Milestone: v0.4.0 Binance Sandbox Product Foundation
Priority: P0
Owner role: Adapter & Integration Agent
Review role: Verification & Release Gatekeeper
Risk: medium

## Goal

Wire a deterministic Binance fixture market data replay path for v0.4.

Plain language: before talking about orders or strategy behavior, v0.4 needs a
repeatable Binance data stream that runs locally.

## Dependencies

- `V04-004`

## Scope

- Add or harden a fixture-backed Binance replay path.
- Keep the replay deterministic and local.
- Make the replay usable by later strategy smokes and dashboard state.
- Record the supported replay inputs and known limits.

## Non-goals

- Do not require real exchange credentials.
- Do not claim production market-data parity.
- Do not turn replay into a real-time live feed product.

## Related files

- `crates/adapters/binance/` (TO_MODIFY): replay and fixture path.
- `tests/` (TO_MODIFY): deterministic replay coverage.

## Implementation guidance

Favor determinism over feature breadth. The replay path must be strong enough
to drive EMA, RSI, mock execution, and risk smoke without needing network
access.

## Verification criteria

- Binance replay runs from checked-in fixtures.
- Replay is deterministic across repeated local runs.
- Replay outputs are usable by later v0.4 smokes.
