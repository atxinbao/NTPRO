## DTO and Validation Summary

- Added `V04SandboxStrategyConfig` as the stable v0.4 sandbox strategy DTO.
- Added `V04SandboxStrategyName` with `ema` and `rsi`.
- Added `V04EmaSignalMode` with the v0.4 `cross` mode.
- Shared validation requires non-empty `instrument_id`, `bar_type`,
  positive decimal `trade_size`, positive `max_orders`, and
  `risk_profile = "sandbox"`.
- EMA validation requires `fast_period`, `slow_period`, and `signal_mode`;
  `slow_period` must be greater than `fast_period`, and warmup defaults to
  `slow_period`.
- RSI validation requires `period`, `oversold_threshold`, and
  `overbought_threshold`; thresholds must be in `0.0..=1.0`, oversold must be
  less than overbought, and warmup defaults to `period`.
- CLI sandbox config now validates the strategy DTO and includes strategy
  label/warmup in validate/run output and artifacts.

## Behavior Impact
