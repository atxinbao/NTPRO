The relevant contract is in `DataClient::connect`: live startup may cancel a
pending connect future when stop, shutdown, or interrupt arrives before the
adapter finishes connecting. Adapter implementations must treat cancellation as
a failed connection attempt and must not leave hidden half-connected state.

## Executable Proof

The executable mock proof is in `crates/live/src/node.rs`:

- `ConnectCancellationProbe`
- `ConnectCleanupGuard`
- `CancellationProbeDataClient`
- `test_data_client_connect_future_cleanup_on_stop_request`
- `test_data_client_connect_future_cleanup_on_shutdown_request`

The mock `connect()` future:

1. marks `connect_started`;
2. marks a simulated resource as acquired;
3. marks a simulated half-connected state;
4. installs a cleanup guard;
5. stays pending forever.

When the startup driver observes stop or shutdown, it returns the cancellation
status and drops the pending future. The cleanup guard then proves:

- the connect future was dropped;
- simulated resources were released;
- half-connected state was cleared;
- the client did not report connected.
