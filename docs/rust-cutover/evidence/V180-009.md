## Summary

V180-009 adds a read-only Dashboard panel for v0.18 cancel recovery evidence.
The panel reads local supervisor artifacts from `artifact_root/v0_18/` and
surfaces cancel preview, risk gate, owner approval, response redaction,
post-cancel readback, incident/audit closeout, remaining risk, and artifact
paths.

Plain Chinese summary: 这个任务把撤单恢复准备状态展示到 Dashboard。大白话：页面能看撤单预览、
风险门禁、Owner 审批、回查、审计和剩余风险，但不能点撤单、不能下单，也没有控制端点。
