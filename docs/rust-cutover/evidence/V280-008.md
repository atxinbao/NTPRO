## Summary

V280-008 closes the v0.28.0 backend closure fail-closed hardening layer with a
deterministic source-controlled artifact and replay validator. The replay
accepts ready backend closure while keeping trading readiness `not_ready`,
degrades partial and stale closure states, and fails closed for missing
components, malformed evidence, source drift, product-ready claims, forbidden
controls, and missing required-false boundaries. The v0.28.0 backend closure
readiness matrix now records `backend_closure_fail_closed_hardening` as
`runtime-closed`.

Plain Chinese summary: 本任务把 v0.28 backend closure 的 fail-closed 汇总层固化为
可复放 artifact。它证明 partial/stale closure 只能 degraded，missing component、
malformed evidence、source drift、product-ready claim、forbidden control、missing
required-false boundary 都会 fail closed。runtime closure health 仍然和 trading
readiness 分离；本任务不开放任何交易操作能力。
