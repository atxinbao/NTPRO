## Summary

V240-006 adds the v0.24.0 retry / no-retry policy ledger contract, golden trace
coverage, release replay scope entries, and fail-closed verifier. The ledger
records explicit retry preview evidence for transport and timeout cases,
terminal no-retry evidence for business and risk rejections, and blocking
evidence for duplicate retry, missing prior attempt, unknown state, and policy
mismatch.

Plain Chinese summary: 本任务只新增 retry / no-retry policy ledger 证据。它
证明 retry 默认不能隐式发生；transport/timeout 也必须有 policy、scope、prior
attempt、approval 和 audit 才能生成只读 preview；business/risk rejection 是
terminal no-retry；duplicate、missing prior、unknown state、policy mismatch 都会
fail closed。
