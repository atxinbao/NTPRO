## Context

The High impact register had 10 `OPEN` items before DRG-008. DRG-008 re-ran or
inspected every item:

- five tests fail with concrete assertion or timeout evidence;
- four risk tests are empty placeholder functions and cannot be accepted as
  regression evidence even though they pass with `--ignored`;
- one dYdX adapter test still times out during subscription restoration.

Because none of the 10 items has valid passing regression coverage, DRG-008 did
not remove any `#[ignore]` attribute.
