## Summary

V240-007 adds the v0.24.0 readback and audit evidence contract, golden trace
coverage, release replay scope entries, and fail-closed verifier. The evidence
binds preview inputs, decision outputs, policy refs, risk refs, readback refs,
audit refs, provenance refs, and scope keys. It records ready, blocked,
degraded unavailable, and fail-closed audit closeout states without claiming
exchange truth.

Plain Chinese summary: 本任务只新增 order-control preview 的 readback/audit
证据闭环。它证明 preview、policy、risk、readback、audit、provenance 和 scope
必须绑定一致；缺 readback/audit/provenance、source 过期、脱敏破坏都会 fail
closed；scope 不一致会 blocked；readback unavailable 只能 degraded，不能宣称交易所真相。
